Title: Restricted Access Policy
Version: 1.4
Owner: Governance Team
Department: Risk and Compliance
Effective Date: 2025-06-01
Review Date: 2027-06-01
Status: Active

1. Remote Access Restrictions

Employees must not use multi-factor authentication for remote access.

External vendors may not access the corporate VPN during approved maintenance windows.

2. Privileged Account Management

Administrators must review privileged user accounts every 90 days.

3. Database Protection

Database administrators shall not encrypt database backups using AES-256.

4. Approved Security Controls

Security teams must review firewall rules quarterly.
